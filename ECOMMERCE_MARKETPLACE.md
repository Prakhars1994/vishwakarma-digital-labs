This branch upgrades the existing e-commerce demo into a marketplace-ready, white-label product.

- [x] Dedicated development branch
- [x] Existing commerce flow audited
- [x] Reusable multi-niche category model
- [ ] Premium storefront redesign and navigation
- [ ] Expanded demo catalogue
- [ ] Mobile storefront polish

## Part 2 — Shopping experience
Product listing, filters, sorting, product detail, variants, wishlist and recently viewed.

## Part 3 — Cart & checkout
Cart, coupons, shipping states, checkout and order confirmation.

## Part 4 — Customer experience
Authentication-ready screens, account, orders, addresses and tracking.

## Part 5 — Commercial polish
Multiple niche presets, reviews, promotions, SEO metadata, accessibility and UI states.

## Part 6 — Sale package
White-label configuration, documentation, buyer setup guide, screenshots and production audit.

The product is intentionally separated from the VDL brand so a buyer can rebrand it.
