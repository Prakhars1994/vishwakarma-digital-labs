# The Cost of Inaction: Why Slow Websites Are Killing Your Growth

Let me do the math for you.

Your website is slow. Let's say it takes 8 seconds to load.

## What That Means in Real Numbers

**Every second = 7% more visitors leave**

8 seconds × 7% = 56% of your visitors bounce before your site even fully loads.

Let's break this down:

**If you get 1,000 visitors per month:**
- You're actually only getting 440 engaged visitors
- You're losing 560 potential customers every single month
- At 2% conversion rate: 560 × 2% = 11.2 lost customers
- At ₹50,000 per customer: **₹5.6 lakhs lost monthly**

That's **₹67.2 lakhs per year.**

And that's just from slow loading time.

---

## The Multiplier Effect of Multiple Problems

But your website probably has other problems too:

**1. No Mobile Optimization**
- 70% of your traffic is mobile
- If it's not optimized: another 30% bounce
- Additional loss: **₹2-3 lakhs monthly**

**2. Complex Checkout**
- 70% of carts are abandoned
- If checkout isn't streamlined: another 30% abandonment
- Additional loss: **₹2 lakhs monthly**

**3. No Follow-Up Automation**
- 98% of visitors don't buy on first visit
- Without follow-up sequences: you convert 0 of them
- Additional loss: **₹4-5 lakhs monthly**

**4. Poor Design**
- 75% judge credibility by design
- Outdated design = 40% lower conversion
- Additional loss: **₹2 lakhs monthly**
